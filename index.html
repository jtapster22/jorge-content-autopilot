How it works

Three steps, all through TikTok's official developer platform. The app never touches any account other than Jorge's own.

  1. 1

    Sign-in with Login Kit

    Jorge authorizes the app on TikTok's own sign-in screen. The app receives an access token scoped to his account only — it cannot see his password, and he can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings.

  2. 2

    Upload his own video video.upload

    A finished video file from Jorge's own content workflow is uploaded to his TikTok inbox as a draft, where he can review it in the TikTok app before it goes live.

  3. 3

    Direct post video.publish

    When Jorge chooses, the app publishes the video directly to @jorgethecoach. Every post is flagged as AI-generated content (is_aigc) and marked as promoting his own business (brand_organic), because the videos are produced with AI tools for his own brand.

What it does — and doesn't do

The scope is deliberately narrow. This is not a public product, a scheduling service for other creators, or a growth tool.

It does

  • Post and draft Jorge's own videos on @jorgethecoach
  • Use only TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API
  • Flag every post as AI-generated, own-business content
  • Run only when Jorge runs it — no posting on anyone else's behalf

It doesn't

  • Access, post to, or read any other user's account or data
  • Scrape TikTok, buy engagement, or automate likes, follows, or comments
  • Collect data from viewers or followers
  • Sell, rent, or share any data with third parties
AI disclosure: Jorge's videos are created with AI assistance. The app sets TikTok's AI-generated content flag on every direct post so viewers and TikTok's review systems always see that disclosure.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Last updated November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Jorge Content Autopilot (the “App”) accesses, why, and how it is handled. The short version: the App serves exactly one user — its operator — touches only his own TikTok account and his own videos, and collects nothing from anyone else.

1Who this policy covers

The App's only user is its operator, Jorge, acting on his own TikTok account, @jorgethecoach. The App has no sign-up, no public accounts, no audience-facing features, and no access to data about TikTok viewers, followers, or any other person.

2Information the App accesses

  • TikTok authorization data. When the operator signs in with TikTok Login Kit, TikTok issues an access token and a refresh token, along with the account's Open ID and basic profile information (such as display name and username) needed to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video files. The video files the operator chooses to publish, plus the title/caption he provides for each post. These are sent to TikTok through the Content Posting API.
  • Post status responses. TikTok's API responses confirming whether an upload or post succeeded, so the operator can verify his own posts.

The App does not access direct messages, comments, follower lists, watch history, or any other user's data, and it requests no permissions beyond what posting requires (video.publish, video.upload, and basic sign-in profile access).

3How the information is used

Authorization data is used only to authenticate the App to TikTok's API on the operator's behalf. Video files and captions are used only to create the posts and drafts the operator requests. Post status responses are used only to confirm results to the operator. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

4Sharing

Data is sent to exactly one place: TikTok's official API, which is the entire function of the App. No data is sold, rented, shared with, or disclosed to any other third party. There are no advertising partners, analytics providers, or data brokers.

5Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens are stored in access-controlled credential storage on the operator's own systems, never in this public page, source code, or any public repository. Video files are held only as part of the operator's own content library. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored credentials, though no method of storage is completely secure.

6Retention and deletion

Tokens are retained only while the App remains authorized. The operator can end all access at any time by revoking the App in TikTok's settings (Settings → Manage app permissions) or by deleting the stored credentials, after which the App can no longer reach the account. Published videos remain on TikTok under TikTok's own policies and the operator's control of his account. To request deletion of any data held by the App, contact the address in Section 9.

7Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. The App itself contains no advertising or third-party tracking technology.

8Children

The App is a personal tool for its adult operator and is not directed at children. It collects no information from children or from any member of the public.
